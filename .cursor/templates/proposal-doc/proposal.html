13. Terms & Conditions — Development Engagement

13.1 Relationship

{{Developer}} provides the services in this proposal to {{Client}}. Each party handles its own taxes. Withholding only if the law requires it.

13.2 Intellectual property

After the full project fee is received, the client owns the custom deliverables. The developer keeps pre-existing tools and generic components. Portfolio use unless the client opts out in writing. Third-party licenses stay with their vendors.

13.3 Confidentiality

Non-public information stays confidential. The obligation survives 3 years after termination.

13.4 Payment terms

The fee and schedule are Section 6. A billing statement per installment. Work or handover may wait until an open amount is cleared. Project-fee payments are non-refundable.

13.5 Payment & timeline dependency

Work starts when the kickoff payment is received, not when this proposal is signed. Handover follows clearance of the remaining fee.

13.6 Timeline schedule conditions

  • The schedule starts at kickoff.
  • It moves if client inputs are late.
  • Work may pause if an installment or a prerequisite is still open.

13.7 Client responsibilities

The client supplies materials and a primary contact. UAT feedback is due within 3 business days. Client delay extends the timeline under 13.6.

13.8 Change control

Work outside Section 3 needs a written change order before it starts.

13.9 Warranty

Deliverables match Section 3 for {{N}} calendar days from final handover. Defects on existing scope only.

13.10 Limitation of liability

This is a software build, not a guarantee of business outcomes. Liability is capped at fees actually paid, except for confidentiality breaches or willful misconduct.

13.11 Termination

Either party may terminate with 14 days written notice. Amounts already paid are not refunded. Completed artifacts follow within 7 business days after amounts due are paid.

13.12 Acceptance & handover

Criteria are Section 12. Deemed accepted if there is no written rejection with specific defects within 5 business days. Handover items are held until the final payment is cleared.

13.13 End-user legal content

The developer implements consent screens. The client writes end-user terms and privacy text and is the personal-information controller.

13.14 End-user contact — Developer not customer-facing

End users are not sent to the developer. The client’s primary contact reports defects.

13.15 Governing law & disputes

Republic of the Philippines. 30 days of good-faith negotiation, then Philippine courts.
